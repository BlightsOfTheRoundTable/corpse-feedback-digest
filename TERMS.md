# Terms of service: Corpse Digest bot

The Corpse Digest bot is a private, read-only tool for the Corpse Discord
server. It can't be added to other servers.

- **What it does:** it reads the feedback and bug-report channels and
  sends a summary to the developers. See [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md) for what
  that involves.
- **No commands:** you don't interact with it, and it never posts, replies
  or DMs anyone.
- **No warranty:** it's provided as is. Summaries are written by an AI model
  and may get things wrong. They help the developers read feedback, but they
  don't decide anything.
- **Discord's rules apply:** using the Corpse server means following
  Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines, and the server's own
  rules.
- **Changes:** these terms may change. Changes are made in this repository,
  so its history shows every change.
