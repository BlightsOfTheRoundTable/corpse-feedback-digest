# Corpse, for the digest

Corpse is a necromancer roguelite (a horde survivors-like) played in the
browser on itch.io, in beta. The necromancer raises a horde from corpses and
steers it with three postures (Push, Ball, Spread) to save a village from
waves of the dead and its boss.

## Villages (each has its own enemies and boss)

- **Mire's End**: a fishing village; the tide rises each wave and swallows the
  map; boss: the Leviathan (dives, tidal waves).
- **Starvecrow**: a starving farm; crops block sight and movement; scarecrows
  spawn crows, harvesters sweep; boss: the Gleaner (eats your horde).
- **Lychgate**: a monastery; holy ground burns your dead and blocks raising;
  warrior monks, defiled priests; boss: the Cardinal (brimstone).
- **The Deads** (in development): a mine in the dark; boss: the Worm.
- **Coldharbour** (in development): a frozen pass; frost and gusts; boss: the
  Charnel House.

## Systems players talk about

- Raising: the raise beat (every 10 s), minion types on the D-pad, command cap.
- Minions: skeleton, flesh ogre, wraith, drowned sailor, siren, scarecrow,
  warrior monk, defiled priest, tunnelling miner, Laid-By.
- Postures: Push, Ball, Spread.
- Spells (Grave Bolt, Consume Essence, Harvest Energy, Sinkhole, Deny the
  Grave, Corpse Explosion, Rime).
- Upgrade cards between waves, re-rolls; relics (earned by feats, two
  equipped); the veil-vendor (veil-touched souls).
- Veterans (touched minions entombed in the crypt, raised by a card, they gain
  XP and grow mutations).
- Meta: the crypt hall, the cabinet (loadout), the altar, the map and its
  village drawer, the victory screen.
- Tech: controls (keyboard/mouse, controller), browser performance, saves.
